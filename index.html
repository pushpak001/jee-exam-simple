General instructions

  • Total duration of the examination is 120 minutes. The countdown timer at the top right shows the time left. When it reaches zero, the exam ends and is submitted automatically.
  • The question palette on the right shows the status of each question using these symbols:
1 You have not visited the question yet.
3 You have not answered the question.
5 You have answered the question.
7 You have not answered the question, but have marked it for review.
9 You have answered the question and marked it for review. It will be evaluated.
  • Choose one option per question. To change an answer, select another option or click Clear Response.
  • Click Save & Next to save your answer and move on. Answers are counted only when saved.
  • Marking: +4 for a correct answer, −1 for a wrong answer, 0 if unattempted or only marked for review.
  • Switch between Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics using the tabs at the top.
  • Cheating rule: leaving the exam tab or window is recorded. Warnings are shown, and after 3 switches your test is submitted automatically and flagged. Copy, paste and right-click are disabled.
  • After submission your marks are saved and you are logged out automatically.
